ENVIRONMENTAL TELEMETRY

CO₂e Methodology & Data Sources

Scientific and engineering basis for LLM emission estimation

1. Estimation Model & Core Factor

Antigravity Token Dashboard uses an emissions factor of 0.25 grams of CO₂ equivalent per 1,000 tokens processed (0.00025 g CO₂e / token).

CO₂e (grams) = (Input Tokens + Cached Tokens + Output Tokens) × 0.00025

2. Primary Data Sources & Citations

  • Patterson et al. (Google Research & UC Berkeley, 2021/2022): "Carbon Emissions and Large Neural Network Training & Inference". Analyzed real-world energy footprints in Google TPU/GPU datacenters operating at an average Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) of ~1.10.
  • Luccioni, Viguier, & Strubell (Hugging Face, CMU, 2023): "Estimating the Carbon Footprint of BLOOM and Large Language Models". Established that multi-tenant LLM inference generates approximately 0.2g to 0.4g CO₂e per 1k tokens under typical cloud grid carbon intensity.
  • Neuland TokenDashboard Environmental Methodology: Cross-provider benchmarking standard for developer tooling telemetry (GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, Antigravity IDE).

3. Real-World Equivalents Reference

🚗 Passenger Car ~120g CO₂e per km European Environment Agency (EEA) standard petrol vehicle
📱 Smartphone Charge ~8.3g CO₂e per full charge EPA Greenhouse Gas Equivalencies (approx 15Wh grid draw)
🎬 Video Streaming ~36g CO₂e per hour IEA / Carbon Trust average cloud video transmission
